# Code of Conduct

We follow the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful. Give feedback that helps. Do not harass, insult, or discriminate against anyone. Assume good intent, and ask when you are unsure.

Maintainers can remove comments, issues, and pull requests that break these rules, and can ban people who repeat the behavior.

## Reporting

To report a problem, contact the maintainer, [@gruckion](https://github.com/gruckion), through a private message on GitHub. Reports are kept private.
